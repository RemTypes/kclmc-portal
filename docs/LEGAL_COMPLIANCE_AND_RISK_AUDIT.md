## 1. Statutory ICO Data Protection Exemption Self-Assessment

### A. Background & Legal Context
Under the **Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018**, organisations in the UK that process personal data must pay an annual data protection fee to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** unless they qualify for a statutory exemption.

Failure to pay without a documented exemption can lead to an ICO administrative penalty of up to £4,000.

| **5. Charity Umbrella Entity** | KCLMC is an accredited constituent society of **KCLSU** (Charity No. 1136043). KCLSU is already registered as an overarching Data Controller with the ICO. | **PASS** |

### C. Legal Determination & Audit Conclusion

## 2. Lawsuit Mitigation & Negligence Defense Protocol

Under English tort law (*Donoghue v Stevenson*, *Caparo v Dickman*), club committee members, trip leaders, and belayers owe a legal **Duty of Care** to participants. In the event of a mountaineering injury or dispute, liability turns on whether there was a **breach of the standard of care (negligence)**.

### A. The Statutory Non-Exclusion Rule (UCTA 1977 & CRA 2015)
* **The Common Mistake**: Writing blanket disclaimers such as *"KCLMC accepts no liability for death or injury whatsoever."*
* **The Law**: Under **Section 2(1) of the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (UCTA)** and **Section 65 of the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (CRA)**, **any clause that purports to exclude liability for death or personal injury resulting from negligence is void and legally ineffective**.
* **The KCLMC Legal Shield**:
  1. Our Terms of Service explicitly acknowledge statutory liability for negligence.
  2. We bind participants to the **BMC Participation Statement**, establishing the common law defense of **Voluntary Assumption of Risk (*volenti non fit injuria*)** for all inherent, non-negligent mountain hazards (spontaneous rockfall, unpredictable weather, alpine objective dangers).
  3. We maintain an airtight standard of care to defeat any allegation of negligence.

### B. Evidentiary Standards to Defeat Negligence Claims

## 3. Disability Support & Paraclimbing Field Guide (Equality Act 2010)

Under Part 2, Part 7, and Section 101 of the **Equality Act 2010**, KCLMC has a positive legal obligation to provide **Reasonable Adjustments** to ensure disabled members are not placed at a substantial disadvantage.

### A. Paraclimbing Communication Protocols

#### 1. Blind & Visually Impaired (VI) Climbers
* **The Clockface Positional System**:
  - The caller references all holds relative to a 12-hour clock centered on the climber's sternum:
    - *"Left hand at 10 o'clock, 30cm, positive jug"*
    - *"Right foot at 2 o'clock, 15cm, small smear on volume"*
* **Bluetooth Headsets**:
  - Two-way low-latency audio headsets deployed in loud indoor climbing gyms.
* **Tactile Route Preview**:
  - Orientation time granted on the ground to feel start holds, check bouldering mat seams, and map clipping points.
* **Top-Rope Priority**:
  - Bottom-fed or top-roped configurations prioritized to eliminate sudden swing falls.

#### 2. Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing (HoH) Climbers
* **Codified 3-Tug Tactile Rope Communication**:
  - In high winds, around rock arêtes, or out of earshot, verbal shouts are prohibited in favor of rope tugs:
    - **3 Sharp Tugs**: *"Safe / At Belay / Off Belay"*
    - **2 Sharp Tugs**: *"Slack Required"*
    - **4 Sharp Tugs**: *"Take / Tension"*
* **Universal Line-of-Sight Hand Signals**:
  - Palm flat facing climber: *"Stop / Hold"*
  - Thumb pointing down: *"Lowering"*
  - Wave overhead: *"Safe / Off Belay"*
* **Written & Visual Briefings**:
  - Digital WhatsApp / email briefings provided prior to crag departures.

#### 3. Mute & Non-Verbal Climbers
* **Mountaineering Whistle Codes**:
  - *1 blast*: Stop / Pay attention
  - *3 blasts*: Emergency / Assistance needed
  - *Continuous blast*: Rope command acknowledged
* **Trained Buddy Mentorship**:
  - Paired with an experienced committee member familiar with individual communication cues.
* **Laminated Harness Beta Cards**:
  - Weatherproof emergency card clipped to harness detailing key medical beta and communication preferences.

#### 4. Physical Adaptations & Belay Safety
* **Adaptive Harnesses**: Full-body and chest harnesses accommodated for members with spinal, trunk, or pelvic variations.
* **Mandatory Assisted-Braking Devices**: Petzl Grigri or Beal Birdie mandated for all society belaying to provide mechanical camming redundancy.
* **Ground Ballast / Anchors**: Floor sandbags used to counter weight disparities.

## 4. Technical PPE Inspection & Quarantine Register (PUWER Principles)

To uphold safety standards and avoid equipment liability:

1. **Gear Officer Log**: Every club rope, harness, helmet, and belay device carries a unique serial number logged in the KCLMC Gear Inventory.
2. **Periodic Inspection Schedule**:
   - *Ropes*: Tactile core check every month for sheath slippage or core herniation. Retired after 5 years or immediately following a severe factor 1+ fall.
   - *Harnesses & Slings*: Inspected for stitching fraying, chemical contamination, or abrasion.
   - *Helmets*: Checked for shell cracks, foam compression, or webbing tears.
3. **Quarantine Procedure**: Any item subject to a high-energy drop, rockfall impact, or suspected defect is **immediately tagged with red tape, quarantined from the gear store, and permanently destroyed/retired**.

## 5. Safeguarding & Under-18 Protocol (Protection of Freedoms Act 2012)

King's College London occasionally admits students who are 16 or 17 years old at the start of their degree:

1. **Day Meets & Indoor Climbing**: 16–17-year-olds may attend regular London climbing sessions under the standard student code of conduct.
2. **Residential & Overnight Meets (Bunkhouses, Scotland, Alps)**:
   - Under the *Protection of Freedoms Act 2012* and *KCLSU Safeguarding Policy*, **written parental/guardian consent is mandatory** prior to boarding transport.
   - Separate bunkhouse sleeping arrangements or chaperoned rooms are enforced.
   - Committee trip leaders maintain designated Welfare contact information.
